本アプリは、利用者の情報を集めません。氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号・位置情報・端末の識別子（広告IDなど）を集めることはありません。

本アプリは13歳未満の子どもを主な対象としていません。

Privacy Policy (Poi-Sute Booster)

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Black Honey ("we") explains how the mobile game "Poi-Sute Booster" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

None. The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers (such as the advertising ID).

Your progress (floors reached, endless record, collected diamonds, unlocked skills, enemy book) and settings (such as sound on/off) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or any third party.

The App is not directed at children under 13.
